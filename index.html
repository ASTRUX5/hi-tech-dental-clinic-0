FAQ

Common Questions

Is root canal treatment really painful?

Modern RCT with proper local anaesthesia is no more painful than a regular filling. Most patients report feeling pressure but no pain during the procedure.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome. However, booking in advance via WhatsApp is recommended to avoid waiting. Open Mon–Sat, 10AM–1PM and 5–9PM.

How long does teeth whitening last?

Professional whitening typically lasts 12–24 months depending on lifestyle. We provide aftercare kits to help you maintain your bright smile for longer.

Are dental implants safe and long-lasting?

Absolutely. We use internationally certified titanium implants with excellent osseointegration records. With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
