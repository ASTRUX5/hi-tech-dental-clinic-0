FAQ

Common Questions

Is root canal treatment really painful?
Modern root canal treatment with proper local anaesthesia is no more painful than a regular filling. Most patients report feeling pressure but no pain. Our rotary endodontic system makes the procedure faster and far more comfortable than traditional methods.
Do you accept walk-in patients?
Yes, walk-ins are welcome! However, we strongly recommend booking in advance via WhatsApp or our website to avoid waiting times. We're open Monday–Saturday, 10AM–1PM and 5–9PM.
How long does teeth whitening last?
Professional whitening results typically last 12–24 months, depending on lifestyle habits (coffee, tea, smoking). We provide aftercare kits and maintenance tips to help you keep your smile bright for as long as possible.
Are dental implants safe and permanent?
Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. We use internationally certified titanium implants with excellent osseointegration rates. With proper care, they can last a lifetime. Success rates exceed 95% in healthy individuals.
What payment options are available?
We accept cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards, and offer EMI options for major treatments. We believe quality dental care should be accessible — we'll never surprise you with hidden charges.
